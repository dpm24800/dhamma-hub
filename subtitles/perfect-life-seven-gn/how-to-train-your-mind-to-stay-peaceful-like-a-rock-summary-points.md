# How to Train Your Mind to Stay Peaceful Like a Rock — 15-Point Summary

1. **The definition:** a strong mindset is being unshaken by praise and blame, like a rock unmoved by the storm (the Buddha's *Dhammapada* image).
2. **Accept life's ups and downs:** the eight worldly conditions (*loka dhamma* — praise/blame, gain/loss, fame/disgrace, pleasure/pain) come to everyone and must be accepted.
3. **Tip One — stay stable:** in criticism and tough times, be mindful of your mind, emotions, and feelings, and don't chase them.
4. **Self-awareness is essential:** mindfully watching your reactions preserves emotional balance when life throws lemons at you.
5. **Tip Two — build resilience:** resilience is falling, then standing up again as quickly as possible.
6. **Know what's in your control:** others' opinions, words, and thoughts about you are not in your control; your reaction to them always is.
7. **Your reaction is a choice:** pausing to ask "should I get angry? should I hold this negativity?" keeps you from absorbing it.
8. **Worry only about what you can change:** you can change your choices, not other people.
9. **Problem-solving mindset:** stay calm when facing problems so you can reach their root, rather than panicking or becoming your problem.
10. **Remember impermanence:** what happens to you will change, so set the problem aside and investigate its root to solve it.
11. **Tip Three — self-love and self-compassion:** if you truly love yourself, you refuse to take in or share others' negativity.
12. **Daily morning practice:** affirm that no matter what happens, you will love yourself and take no negativity — emptying your mental trash bin daily.
13. **Anger is weakness, not strength:** being easily triggered or displaying negativity is not a strong mindset.
14. **Stop overthinking:** realize it doesn't help; use mindfulness of breathing or the body to return to the present moment (sad→past, anxious→future, peaceful→present).
15. **Counter negative "what ifs"** with positive ones, accept imperfection (be your best, not perfect), take risks, and act — building a mind unshaken by the storms of life.
